# My Favorite Movie: Your Name

## About the Movie

**Your Name** (*Kimi no Na wa*) is a Japanese animated romance-fantasy film released in **2016**. It was directed by Makoto Shinkai and animated by CoMix Wave Films.

## Story Summary

Two teenagers, Mitsuha and Taki, live in different parts of Japan. One day they start *swapping bodies* without knowing why, and they slowly build a bond through notes they leave for each other.

> A beautiful story about connection, distance, and fate.
